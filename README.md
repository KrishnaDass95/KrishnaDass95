# Krishna Dass

**Quality engineer for systems that are hard to test.**

Nine years in QA, eight of them owning quality for a consumer GPS bike computer — 158,000+ devices shipped across three hardware generations. My work sits where firmware, embedded Android, wireless sensors and cloud sync meet, which is also where ordinary test automation quietly stops working.

---

### What I actually do

- Regression-test **BLE and ANT+ sensor pairing** against radio firmware releases — connection and re-pairing, drop-out behaviour at range, value accuracy under load, persistence across device reboot
- Drive **hardware-in-the-loop rigs** that broadcast synthetic sensor traffic to a real device under test
- Validate **GPS navigation and ride behaviour** through mocked route simulation across the full route lifecycle
- Automate Android devices with **Appium and Pytest against physical hardware**, not emulators — sessions attach to a device and drive it by intent rather than launching a single APK
- Test **BLE bridging** between a device and its iOS/Android companion apps with no network connectivity on the device
